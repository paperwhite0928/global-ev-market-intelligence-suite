### 1.2 The 83.5M~90.0M Zero-Sum Reality
Global New Vehicle Total Industry Volume (TIV) will not experience organic expansion. Due to elevated interest rates, persistent inflation, and vehicle affordability friction, the average vehicle fleet age in North America and Western Europe has expanded to an **unprecedented 12.5 years**. In this environment, unit growth by one player demands the direct margin cannibalization of another.

## 3. Methodological Calibration: Eliminating 5 Data Biases

To ensure decision-grade rigor, this framework rejects raw 2020~2023 linear projections and calibrates for five empirical distortions:

1. **Pandemic Outlier Correction**: Removes the distorted 2020~2022 early-adopter EV demand bubble driven by zero interest rates and stimulus checks.
2. **Chinese Non-Market Distortions**: De-links Beijing's state-subsidized vehicle purchasing quotas from free-market consumer elasticity curves.
3. **Downside Price Stickiness**: Accounts for why falling battery cell costs ($151 ➔ $85/kWh) fail to lower retail vehicle MSRPs due to fixed mining off-take contracts, tariffs, and rising labor overhead.
4. **Fleet Age Expansion (12.5 Years)**: Adjusts total addressable volume downwards from 90.0M to **83.5M units**, reflecting prolonged replacement cycles.
5. **Non-Linear Discrete Policy Shocks**: Models regulatory transitions as sudden, step-function disruptions rather than smooth statistical curves.
